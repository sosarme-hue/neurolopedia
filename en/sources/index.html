Sources

Sources and scientific library

Institutional resources, scientific societies, medicines agencies, databases and patient associations where you can find out more and check what you read.

Databases help you find evidence, but they are not evidence in themselves. A reliable source does not replace your healthcare team: if a symptom, a result or a news story worries you, talk to your GP practice about it.

Recent guidelines and documents (2024-2026)

Clinical practice guidelines, diagnostic criteria and agency decisions published between 2024 and 2026, each one checked individually. Each also appears on the pages it affects.

Stroke

  • Prabhakaran S, et al. 2026 Guideline for the Early Management of Patients With Acute Ischemic Stroke (AHA/ASA). Stroke. 2026. Link ↗ · the American guideline on acute ischaemic stroke, replacing the 2018-2019 version.
  • Bushnell C, et al. 2024 Guideline for the Primary Prevention of Stroke (AHA/ASA). Stroke. 2024;55:e344-e424. Link ↗ · preventing a first stroke.
  • Wardlaw JM, et al. ESO guideline on cerebral small vessel disease, part 2: lacunar ischaemic stroke. Eur Stroke J. 2024;9:5-68. Link ↗ · lacunar infarcts and small vessel disease.
  • Steiner T, Al-Shahi Salman R, et al. ESO/EANS guideline on the management of spontaneous intracerebral haemorrhage. Eur Stroke J. 2025. Link ↗ · the European guideline on brain haemorrhage, replacing the 2014 version.
  • Etminan N, Vergouwen MDI, et al. ESO/EANS/ESMINT guideline on aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhage. Eur Stroke J. 2026. Link ↗ · the European guideline on subarachnoid haemorrhage from an aneurysm.
  • Spanish Ministry of Health. Stroke Strategy of the National Health System. 2024 update. Link ↗ · the Spanish plan: prevention, stroke code, stroke units and rehabilitation (in Spanish).

Dementia and Alzheimer's disease

  • Jack CR Jr, et al. Revised criteria for diagnosis and staging of Alzheimer's disease: Alzheimer's Association Workgroup. Alzheimers Dement. 2024. Link ↗ · biological diagnostic criteria and staging.
  • Alzheimer's Association. Clinical practice guideline for blood-based biomarkers in the diagnostic workup of Alzheimer's disease. Alzheimers Dement. 2025. Link ↗ · when to use blood tests in diagnosis.
  • Livingston G, et al. Dementia prevention, intervention, and care: 2024 report of the Lancet standing Commission. Lancet. 2024;404:572-628. Link ↗ · 14 modifiable risk factors.
  • European Medicines Agency. Leqembi (lecanemab, 2025) and Kisunla (donanemab, 2025): European public assessment reports. Link ↗ · European authorisation and its restrictions.
  • NICE. Lecanemab and donanemab for treating mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia caused by Alzheimer's disease. 2025. Link ↗ · the UK assessment of cost and benefit.

Epilepsy

  • Beniczky S, et al. Updated classification of epileptic seizures: position paper of the ILAE. Epilepsia. 2025;66:1804-23. Link ↗ · the new classification of epileptic seizures.
  • NICE. Epilepsies in children, young people and adults (NG217). Updated January 2025. Link ↗ · the UK guideline on diagnosis and treatment, with medicine safety warnings.

Headache

  • Ornello R, et al. Guidelines for the pharmacological treatment of migraine (SISC/IHS). Cephalalgia. 2025;45(4). Link ↗ · the international guideline on medicines for migraine.
  • Charles AC, et al. CGRP-targeting therapies are a first-line option for the prevention of migraine: an American Headache Society position statement update. Headache. 2024. Link ↗ · anti-CGRP medicines as a first-line option.

Multiple sclerosis, ALS and neuromuscular disease

  • Montalban X, et al. Diagnosis of multiple sclerosis: 2024 revisions of the McDonald criteria. Lancet Neurol. 2025;24:850-65. Link ↗ · new diagnostic criteria (optic nerve, central vein sign, kappa free light chains).
  • Van Damme P, et al. European Academy of Neurology (EAN) guideline on the management of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. Eur J Neurol. 2024;31:e16264. Link ↗ · the European ALS guideline.
  • European Medicines Agency. Qalsody (tofersen): authorisation under exceptional circumstances. 2024. Link ↗ · the first treatment for ALS caused by SOD1 mutations.
  • Jacob S, et al. Myasthenia gravis: Association of British Neurologists' management guidelines, 2025 update. Pract Neurol. 2025;25:422-37. Link ↗ · the UK update on treatment.

Other

  • Höglinger GU, et al. A biological classification of Parkinson's disease: the SynNeurGe research diagnostic criteria. Lancet Neurol. 2024. Link ↗ · a proposed biological definition (for research).
  • Winkelman JW, et al. Treatment of restless legs syndrome and periodic limb movement disorder: an AASM clinical practice guideline. J Clin Sleep Med. 2025. Link ↗ · iron and alpha-2-delta ligands first line; dopamine agonists are no longer the preferred choice.
  • Soliman N, et al. Pharmacotherapy and non-invasive neuromodulation for neuropathic pain: a systematic review and meta-analysis (NeuPSIG). Lancet Neurol. 2025;24:413-28. Link ↗ · updated recommendations on treating neuropathic pain.
  • NICE. Meningitis (bacterial) and meningococcal disease: recognition, diagnosis and management (NG240). 2024. Link ↗ · recognition and urgent treatment.
  • Australian and New Zealand guideline for mild traumatic brain injury, concussion and persisting post-concussion symptoms. Med J Aust. 2025;223(9). Link ↗ · guideline on concussion and mild head injury.
  • World Health Organization. Global status report on neurology. 2025. Link ↗ · the worldwide state of neurological care.
