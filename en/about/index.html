About

Neurolopedia grew out of primary care, where headaches, dizziness, memory worries and tingling are part of every working day.

Alejandro Rosàs Navarro

I am a family physician and a specialist in Preventive Medicine and Public Health, with over 38 years in primary care, registered with the Barcelona Medical Association under number 28574.

In my practice, neurology is everywhere: headaches, dizziness, fear of forgetfulness, tingling hands, sciatica, tremor, the person who has had a stroke and their family, or an MRI report nobody has explained. These are very common reasons to see a doctor, and among those with the most fears, myths and miracle products.

Neurolopedia aims to explain clearly how the brain and nervous system work, what happens when they fail, how those conditions are diagnosed and treated, and what to ask at your appointment.

What it is and what it is not

Neurolopedia is a general-audience encyclopedia. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment or interpretation of individual results, and it does not replace care from health professionals. Never change or stop a medicine because of something you read here without asking your clinician.
