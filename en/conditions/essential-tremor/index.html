Essential tremor

The most common movement disorder: shaking of the hands when using them, which often runs in families and improves with a little alcohol. It is not Parkinson's.

Text: Alejandro Rosàs, family physician · Updated: 26 November 2026

General information only. It does not replace advice from a health professional. For sudden neurological symptoms, call emergency services.

In this article

  1. What it is
  2. Symptoms
  3. Diagnosis
  4. Treatment

What it is

Essential tremor is the most common movement disorder: it affects about 1% of the population and more than 5% of people over 65. It causes an action tremor, which appears when using the hands (writing, drinking from a glass, doing up buttons, eating with a spoon) and disappears at rest, exactly the opposite of the tremor of Parkinson's disease.

It can start at any age, with two peaks: in youth and from age 50 onwards. More than half of cases have a family history, with autosomal dominant inheritance, although no single gene has been identified.

Symptoms

  • Tremor of both hands and forearms when holding a posture or moving, often somewhat asymmetrical.
  • It can affect the head ("yes-yes" or "no-no" tremor), the voice (a shaky voice), the jaw or the legs.
  • It gets worse with stress, tiredness, caffeine, cold and some medicines.
  • It improves with small amounts of alcohol in many patients (which is not recommended as a treatment).
  • It progresses very slowly over the years.

Although often considered benign, it can be very disabling and lead to embarrassment and social isolation. Some patients also have mild unsteadiness when walking or mild cognitive problems with age.

Diagnosis

It is clinical: history, family history and examination. The patient is asked to draw a spiral, write or drink from a glass. It is worth ruling out treatable causes of tremor:

  • Hyperthyroidism.
  • Medicines: salbutamol, lithium, valproic acid, antidepressants, excess levothyroxine, corticosteroids.
  • Caffeine, alcohol withdrawal, anxiety.

If there is doubt about Parkinson's, a DaTSCAN is normal in essential tremor.

Treatment

It is only treated if the tremor is bothersome or limiting.

Medicines:

  • Propranolol and primidone are first-line, and reduce tremor by about 50% in half of patients. They can be combined.
  • Alternatives: topiramate, gabapentin, benzodiazepines.
  • Botulinum toxin for head or voice tremor. See botulinum toxin.

Practical measures: weighted wristbands, thicker or electronically stabilised cutlery, cups with lids and straws, resting the elbows.

If the tremor is severe and resistant:

  • Deep brain stimulation of the thalamus (ventral intermediate nucleus): very effective and adjustable.
  • MRI-guided focused ultrasound (HIFU): with no incision, it destroys a point in the thalamus with beams of ultrasound. It is applied to one side and the effect is immediate. Spain is one of the countries with the most experience.
